How this works, and what it can't tell you

What the number is

An experimental index from 0 to 1 comparing a chosen day, or a short stretch, with a period you picked as your usual. It uses robust statistics and only counts changes in the harder direction. It is not a relapse probability, a diagnosis or an emergency alert.

What it isn't

Missing data is never counted as good or bad; it simply isn't scored. A single unusual day is noted, never flagged. Patterns need sustained evidence or slow drift across different domains against the original anchor. The daily baseline adapts slowly within a cap. Context remains visible; original anchored evidence is retained. These heuristic defaults need calibration on real outcomes before any clinical use. Demo people are made up; research participants keep their original observations only.

Why you see so little

Only noteworthy differences are shown up front. The thresholds are presentation choices, not clinical cut-offs. Every measurement is still used and is one click away.
