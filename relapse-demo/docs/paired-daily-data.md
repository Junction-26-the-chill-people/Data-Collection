# Paired daily movement and health examples

The bundle contains 4,948 original PSYCHE-D people (35,694 observed monthly rows, 6,197 missing calendar slots) and 11 separate generated people with 120 days each. No generated GPS is attached to a research participant. No GPS from the separate movement-source explorer is attached to a health record.

Every generated day carries `participantId`, `subjectId`, `date`, `dayKey` (`subjectId:date`) and `timeZone`, matching its parent person. Health values, trips, visits and movement summary belong to that exact record. Trip IDs are scoped to their person/day; use `dayKey` with a trip ID as a globally unique linkage key.

The first eight stories and Mila retain selected-walk coverage: other away-time is unknown and must not be filled with inferred paths. Device-missing days have no routes and missing measurements. Their `movementSummary.scope` is `selected_walking_trips`.

Two additional stories have `movementSummary.scope: complete_generated_day` and a complete 24-hour `locationTimeline`. They use an explicit UTC scenario clock, matching record dates and timestamps; these are generated timelines, not measured all-day GPS. Stay events carry place ID and coordinates; walk events reference the separate trip geometry. Consecutive event times meet exactly with no gaps or overlapping dwell time. The original home-hour and step values are preserved. New itineraries are fitted to that exact home/away budget, with mapped distance bounded by total steps times a 0.72 m stride. Summaries include exact away/home seconds and mapped distance. Each person has a distinct neighborhood and four fictional public-network place roles, while generated home markers do not identify anyone's residence.

- `sim-09`, Sofia: one difficult day on **2026-03-10 (Day 065, index 64)**, with 2.8 hours of sleep, 350 steps, no away trips and 24 hours at home. Normal routines return on 2026-03-11. The day signal remains high, but model 2.0 reports `noted` / `no_change`; the next day is `steady`. One day is never a pattern flag. These are illustrative behavior-change scores, not validated relapse probabilities or labels.
- `sim-10`, Elias: weekday and weekend itineraries visit different public-network stops, with longer park/errand stays. It illustrates a different stable routine with complete generated location coverage.

Rebuild using `python scripts/build_demo_bundle.py --real-input .tmp/all-records/demo-data.json --verify-alerts`. Python builds the data; the optional verification flag invokes the installed Node executable (`--node PATH` can specify it) against the shipped dependency-free engine. This full original input is required to retain all research people. `tests/test_data_bundle.py` checks exact research record preservation, deterministic generation, valid public OSM edges, walking speed, trip ordering, distance/steps consistency, complete timeline duration, home/away budgets, linkage keys, missingness and the isolated-day recovery pattern.


- `sim-11`, Mila: a 120-day slow fade with frozen anchor days 0-27. Her first `flag_drift` occurs on **2026-04-14 (Day 100, index 99)** while the day signal is only 0.23. Activity and mood satisfy the unchanged anchor thresholds and adverse trend tests. No `flag_sustained` occurs anywhere in this generated story, and no pre-Day-71 seven-day span has more than two high days. This is an authored demonstration, not a performance estimate.

Mila uses Gaussian day noise with steps SD 1,020 (12% of initial 8,500), sleep SD 0.22 h, bedtime SD 0.25 h, mood SD 0.35, home time SD 0.7 h, plus bounded efficiency and heart-rate noise. After day index 28, the step mean falls by **3% of its initial mean per week**, sleep by 0.08 h/week, bedtime rises by 0.10 h/week, mood falls by **0.13 points/week**, and home time rises by 0.25 h/week. Steps and mood rates are modest explicit departures from the initially suggested 2% and 0.12: the unchanged 25% step and robust-z mood thresholds otherwise need longer than the requested visible demo interval. Seed 20261125 was the second candidate checked; it was selected so the unchanged trend test displays the intended example by Day 100. This choice is disclosed and is not clinical calibration, independent validation or an estimate of false-alarm performance.

Mara and Ada never flag under the daily defaults. Noah and Jonas reach `flag_sustained` in their later disruption phases. Lea flags during disruption and returns to `steady`. Emil's confirmed illness can contextualize the sustained path, but the anchor drift path still reports with `contextNote` and later returns to `steady`; context is not proof that deterioration is harmless. Machine-readable outcomes are regenerated in `docs/synthetic-story-checks.json` by `node tests/test_story_outcomes.cjs`.
